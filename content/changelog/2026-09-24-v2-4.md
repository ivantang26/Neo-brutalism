- Clients can now pay part of an invoice. The balance and every payment show on the invoice and in the client portal.
- Ask for a deposit when you send a quote: 25%, 50% or any fixed amount.
- Reminders now mention the remaining balance, not the original total.
- Fixed: invoice PDFs with very long client addresses no longer overflow the page.
